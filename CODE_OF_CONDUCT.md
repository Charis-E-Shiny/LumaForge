# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want LumaForge to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experience levels
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Critique ideas and code, not people
- Credit other people's work
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in an academic or professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/Charis-E-Shiny). Reports are handled as privately as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may issue a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the severity of the behaviour.
